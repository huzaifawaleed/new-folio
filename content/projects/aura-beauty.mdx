## The Problem

Lumina Beauty Clinics was managing all bookings manually via phone calls and Instagram DMs. Potential customers had no way to see service pricing, before/after work, or staff profiles before reaching out — creating friction that cost the clinic bookings.

## The Solution

An elegant, gallery-driven website that lets the work speak for itself. The site features a curated before/after gallery, full services pricing table, staff profiles, and an Instagram feed integration — all leading to a frictionless WhatsApp booking button.

## Key Results

- **2× bookings in 30 days** after launch
- Reduced time spent on manual booking coordination
- Before/after gallery building trust with new clients before they even reach out
- Staff profiles adding a personal touch and helping clients choose their preferred stylist

## What I Built

- Elegant hero section with clinic photography
- Before/after transformation gallery with lightbox
- Services pricing table with categories
- Staff profile cards with specialties
- Instagram feed integration showing recent work
- Smooth animations with Framer Motion
- One-tap WhatsApp booking flow
- Google Reviews showcase section
- Mobile-optimized throughout
