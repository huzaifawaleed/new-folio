## The Problem

Warung Selera was a popular local restaurant relying entirely on walk-in customers and word of mouth. They had no online presence, no way for customers to view their menu before visiting, and no digital ordering channel.

## The Solution

A fast, mobile-first landing page built to convert visitors into customers. The site features a full digital menu with photos and prices, a prominent WhatsApp ordering button, Google Maps integration, and clearly listed operating hours.

The design prioritizes speed — every page loads in under 2 seconds on mobile, ensuring even customers on slower connections have a seamless experience.

## Key Results

- **WhatsApp orders started week one** — customers began placing orders through the site immediately after launch
- Mobile-optimized experience for the restaurant's predominantly mobile audience
- Google Maps integration making it easy for new customers to find the location
- Digital menu reducing phone inquiries and helping customers decide before they arrive
