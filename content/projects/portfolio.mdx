## The Problem

EatBBQ, an all-you-can-eat BBQ and Steamboat buffet restaurant, had no online presence to showcase their dining experience. Without a website, potential customers couldn't find key information — location, pricing, menu, or opening hours — before deciding where to eat.

## The Solution

A bold, appetite-driving website that immediately communicates the EatBBQ experience. The dark, food-forward design uses full-bleed imagery and strong typography to pull visitors in, with all the essential info (menu, pricing, gallery, contact) easy to find within seconds.

## Key Results

- Online presence allowing customers to discover EatBBQ before visiting
- Clear menu and pricing reducing phone inquiries
- Photo gallery showcasing the food and dining atmosphere
- Location and hours section helping new customers plan their visit

## What I Built

- Full responsive landing page with dark, food-forward aesthetic
- Menu and pricing section with clear categories
- Photo gallery of food and dining experience
- Location, hours, and contact section
- Booking/reservation call-to-action
- Mobile-optimized for on-the-go visitors
